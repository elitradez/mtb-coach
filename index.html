The improvement loop

One clip.
One clear fix.

Most tutorials tell you how a perfect bunny hop works. MTB Coach is being built to show what you should change next.

01 / RECORD

Film side-on

Set a phone down in good light with your full body, bike, and landing area visible.

02 / UNDERSTAND

See the real issue

Break the attempt into phases and show one high-confidence observation with annotated evidence.

03 / PROGRESS

Do one drill

Try a focused correction, upload again, and compare attempt one against your next rep.
