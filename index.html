FAQ

Questions riders ask.

What do I need?

Just your phone and your bike. Prop the phone up side-on, a few meters away, so your whole bike and body are in frame. The app walks you through it.

Which skills can I work on?

Wheelies and bunny hops at launch. Manuals, drops and jumps are next.

I can't wheelie at all yet. Is it for me?

Yes. Your first clips will score low, and that's the point. Every session shows you the next thing to work on, from your very first lift.

When can I use it?

We're opening to the waitlist first, ahead of riding season. Join and you'll get an email the moment your spot opens.
